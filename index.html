    The atelier

    Made slowly, worn for years

    Maison Ossane began in Colombo in 2014 with one pattern-cutter, one table and a single jacket. Every piece is still drafted, cut and finished by hand in our Colombo 07 atelier.

    We work with handloom weavers across Sri Lanka and family-run mills in Italy, choose fibres we can trace, and make in small runs so nothing is wasted. Each garment carries the initials of the hands that made it.
